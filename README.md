# Charnia — Infortts Corporate Registry & Swarm Governance

Charnia is the central operational and legal registry for Infortts corporate affairs, organizational hierarchy, strategic branches, corporate records, and swarm governance.

## Naming Domain Alignment
Named after the Ediacaran lifeform *Charnia*, the first fossil discovered in Precambrian rocks, representing foundational, ancestral growth, legal-structural boundaries, and corporate branch structures.

## System Capabilities
1. **Corporate Structuring**: Managing legal agreements, operational departments, and corporate entities.
2. **Dynamic Governance**: Consensus protocols, AI-managed board resolutions, and multi-signature authorization loops.
3. **Intellectual Property Vault**: Registry of algorithmic patents, trademark assets, and product licenses across the swarm.
